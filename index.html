About Us

A Place Where Children Thrive Naturally

BabyZone Montessori is a dedicated early childhood center located in Surkhet, Nepal. We follow the internationally respected Montessori methodology — fostering independence, curiosity, and a deep love for learning in every child from Playgroup to Class 2.

Child-Centered Learning

Every lesson is tailored to the child's natural development pace

Hands-On Activities

Learning through play, exploration, and sensory experiences

Safe & Caring Environment

A warm, inclusive space where every child feels valued

Qualified Teachers

Trained professionals passionate about early childhood education

Our Programs

Classes from PG to Class 2

Age-appropriate, Montessori-based curriculum designed to nurture every stage of early childhood development.

Age 2.5 – 3 yrs

Play Group (PG)

A gentle introduction to school life through sensory play, music, and group activities. Building social skills and school readiness.

  • Sensory & Motor Development
  • Music & Rhymes
  • Socialization Skills
  • Creative Play
Age 3 – 4 yrs

Nursery

Structured learning through play with introduction to letters, numbers, colors and shapes. Building curiosity and love for learning.

  • Pre-Reading Skills
  • Number Concepts
  • Art & Craft
  • Phonics Introduction
Age 4 – 5 yrs

Lower KG (LKG)

Developing pre-academic skills through Montessori materials. Children explore literacy, numeracy, and scientific thinking.

  • Reading Readiness
  • Basic Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies
  • Language Development
Age 5 – 6 yrs

Upper KG (UKG)

Preparing children for primary school with advanced literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking skills using Montessori methods.

  • Advanced Phonics
  • Basic Writing
  • Problem Solving
  • STEAM Concepts
Age 6 – 7 yrs

Class 1

Formal academic learning with Montessori principles. Reading, writing, mathematics and science in an engaging, hands-on environment.

  • Reading & Writing
  • Mathematics
  • Science Exploration
  • Computer Basics
Age 7 – 8 yrs

Class 2

Building on core academic skills with deeper exploration of language, mathematics, social studies and creative arts for well-rounded development.

  • Advanced Reading
  • Mathematics & Reasoning
  • Social Studies
  • Creative Writing
Why Montessori

The Montessori Difference

The Montessori method has been proven worldwide to produce confident, creative, self-directed learners. Here's why BabyZone Montessori stands apart.

🧠

Self-Directed Learning

Children choose their activities, building independence, focus, and intrinsic motivation from the earliest age.

🌿

Natural Development

Learning aligned with each child's natural developmental stage — no rushing, no pressure, just growth.

🤝

Collaborative Environment

Mixed-age classrooms foster peer learning, empathy, and strong social-emotional development.

🎨

Creative Expression

Arts, music, movement, and storytelling are core to our curriculum — nurturing the whole child.

Admission

Simple Admission Process

Getting your child enrolled at BabyZone Montessori is easy. Follow these simple steps:

1

Inquiry

Contact us via phone, email, or visit us in person to learn about our programs and availability.

2

School Visit

Schedule a visit to tour our facilities, meet our teachers, and experience the BabyZone environment.

3

Application

Fill out the admission form with your child's details and submit required documents.

4

Enrollment

Complete enrollment, pay fees, and get your child ready for an amazing learning journey!
